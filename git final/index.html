SAFE SALES IMPORT

Import sales from Excel

Upload a sales register, review each row, then import selected sales. Vehicle details from the spreadsheet are shown in the preview. Only vehicles currently marked In Stock can be sold, and every sale needs a sale price.

Recognizes your Atlas Weekly Report headings too: CHASSIS NUMER, brand, MODEL, acceries, charger, key, card, COLOR, year, type, sold by, cash /bank, Bank and cosutomer Name. Sale Price is still required to record a sale.
